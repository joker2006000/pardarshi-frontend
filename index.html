How PARDARSHI Works

From creating a form to showing transparent records, everything stays connected.

01 — Create Your Organisation

Set up your organisation profile and add the information contributors need.

02 — Create Your Form

Create your own contribution registration form without coding. Add required fields like name and mobile number.

03 — Customize & Publish

Add your logo, images, information and documents, preview the page and publish it when you're ready.

04 — Share With QR & Link

Once published, PARDARSHI generates a unique link and QR code that can be shared or printed.

05 — Receive Contributions

Contributors open the page, provide their information and complete their contribution safely.

06 — Keep Transparent

Contributors can view contribution records, expenses, documents and proofs through your account.
